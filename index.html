Walk is ~3.4 km, south from Kensington Church Street up Portobello, then east to Westbourne.

How we play

Each pub is a hole. Finish the assigned drink in as few sips as possible. Lowest total wins. A down-in-one is a hole-in-one, whatever the par.

Par

  • Pint · par 4
  • Wine · par 3
  • Shot · par 1
  • Cocktail · par 3 · highball par 4
  • Soft drink · same par as the hole’s alcoholic drink

Penalties

  • Spill +1
  • Loo between pubs +1
  • Leave any drink +2

Pace

One drink per pub. Water between holes. Sit one out whenever you like — switch to a soft and keep the card going.

Costume: polos, visors, argyle, ridiculous trousers. Mini club optional, encouraged.
